Cove Media privacy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Cove Media is a player for media servers you already run, like Jellyfin. It has no accounts of its own, no ads and no analytics.

What Cove Media collects

Nothing. The app does not send any information to its developer or to anyone else.

Who the app talks to

Only the server address you type in. Your sign-in, watch history, progress and settings go between your device and that server, the same as with the server's own apps. Whoever runs that server decides how that data is kept.

What stays on your device

The server address, your sign-in token (in the iOS and tvOS Keychain), and small settings like streaming quality and text size. Deleting the app removes them.

Face ID

On servers that support it, the app can sign you in with a passkey. Face ID happens on your device; your face data never leaves it and the app never sees it.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children. What a child can see is set by whoever runs the server.
